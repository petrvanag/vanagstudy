# Surface brief: index.html (landing)

Mode: Persuade. Visitor: a parent of a school student (grades 6-11) deciding whether to trust this school. Action: apply for a diagnostic session. Proof: real teachers with photos and verified olympiad credentials, five student stories, transparent prices, weekly parent reports. Constraints: static HTML/CSS/JS on GitHub Pages, Russian copy preserved from the existing site, no fabricated results. Open: application destination (Telegram contact), domain switch.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is one olympiad problem sheet (listok). It refuses the blue-SaaS school landing page (hero, icon cards, gradient button) and shows thinking instead of claiming it: the hero poses a classic problem with its picture-proof, and a teacher's red pen annotates the sheet.
OWN-WORLD: White printed paper (warm-neutral, faintly tinted), black ink, exactly one red (the teacher's check pen). Serif problem text like a printed textbook with real italics, a Cyrillic-first sans for labels and prices, a Cyrillic pen script only for the red marginal notes. Hairline rules, numbered conditions, margins that carry notes; teacher photos printed in ink-grey like a lyceum yearbook, colour returning on hover.
STORY: The parent sees at once what this is (online school of exact sciences, olympiad route), why it is different (we teach to think; the diagram proves it), and who teaches (winners who did it recently). They believe because the credentials are specific and the prices are plain, and they act through "Zadacha 0": the application for the diagnostic session.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Left, on a sheet margin, a compact masthead (VANAGSTUDY, Shkola tochnykh nauk) and nav with a visible "Записаться на диагностику" action at top right. Large serif H1 "Математика и точные науки без зазубривания" over the lead paragraph and the primary button. Right, a printed problem card: "Докажите, что 1 + 3 + 5 + … + (2n−1) = n²" with a 5x5 square built from L-shaped layers and one red pencil note. Primary action sits directly under the lead.
FORM: Olympiad problem sheet, chosen as the assigned candidate 3 of my ordered list (the roll lands on it), seed key 59402458. Declined challengers: none borrowed beyond one discipline: the darkroom's irreversibility is not used.
